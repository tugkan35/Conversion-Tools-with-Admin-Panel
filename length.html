What is Length?

Length is the measure of how long something is, or the distance from one end of an object to the other. It tells us the size or extent of an object in one dimension.

For example:

  • The length of a pencil could be 15 cm.
  • The length of a football field is about 100 meters.

Length is measured in units like:

  • Meters (m) – standard unit in the metric system
  • Centimeters (cm), millimeters (mm) – for smaller objects
  • Kilometers (km) – for longer distances
  • Inches, feet, yards, miles – used in the imperial system
